BhalooGlassKit.blend  -  open this and adjust it yourself
=========================================================

Double-click BhalooGlassKit.blend, or open Blender and use File > Open.


DO THESE 3 THINGS FIRST
-----------------------
The file may open with the wrong render settings. Check these three:

1. Render Properties (the camera-back icon, left column of tabs)
     Render Engine  ->  CYCLES
   Glass only looks like glass in Cycles. Eevee fakes it and looks flat.

2. Same panel, scroll to  Film  ->  tick  Transparent
   This is what makes the PNG background see-through.

3. Same panel, scroll to  Color Management  ->  View Transform  ->  Standard
   The default is AgX, which deliberately washes out saturated colour.
   This one setting is why my early renders looked like dusty lavender.

Then press F12 to render, and F11 to see the result.


WHAT IS IN THE FILE
-------------------
GLASS_ASSETS        the 10 pieces, laid out down the screen
STUDIO_edit_these   3 light panels + the camera

There is also a text file inside Blender itself: switch an area to the
Text Editor and open READ_ME_FIRST for the same notes.


THE 4 THINGS THAT ACTUALLY CHANGE HOW GLASSY IT LOOKS
------------------------------------------------------
1. THE LIGHT PANELS, more than the material.
   The bright streak on a button IS a reflection of LIGHT_top_streak.
   Move or rotate that panel and the streak moves with it.

   They sit BEHIND the camera deliberately. Looking at a glossy surface
   face-on, you see reflected whatever is behind the lens - so a panel
   placed in front lights the object but never appears in it. That was
   why several of my early attempts had no highlight at all.

   Brightness: select panel > Material Properties > Emission > Strength.

2. THICKNESS. Select a button > Modifier Properties (wrench):
     1_outline_roundness  = how round the ends are
     2_rim_thickness      = how rounded the edge is
   Careful: push 2_rim_thickness past about 0.6 of the object's Z size and
   the top and bottom roundings meet in the middle, leaving a seam line.

3. COLOUR DEPTH is the Volume Absorption node, NOT Base Color.
   Material Properties > scroll down to Volume > Density.
   Higher = deeper, richer glass. This is the difference between
   "coloured glass" and "tinted plastic".

4. If it still looks FLAT, it is nearly always one of these:
     - View Transform still on AgX instead of Standard
     - light panels in front of the camera rather than behind it
     - the object is too thin  (select it, raise Z in the N-panel)
     - render engine still on Eevee


SAVING A PIECE AS PNG
---------------------
Select the piece > View > Frame Selected > move the CAMERA to suit >
F12 > in the render window: Image > Save As > PNG, RGBA.

Send the PNGs back to me and I will crop, size and wire them into the app.


IF THE FILE WILL NOT OPEN
-------------------------
It was written by Blender 5.2. Blender cannot open files newer than
itself. If yours is older, tell me your version number
(Blender > top right, or Help > About) and I will save you one that fits.
